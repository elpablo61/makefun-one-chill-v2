A single sentence of horror, under 200 characters, for the
[One Line, One Chill](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-one-line-one-chill-6d4d5488)
bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.ts` writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(default `/output/entry.txt`).

## The entry

> At 3:12 a.m. Mrs. Hartono knocked twice, the way she always did, and asked me to let her in, though we buried her on Tuesday.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
